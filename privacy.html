DropRun privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026. This is the same text as the policy inside the app (About & privacy).

Your runs stay on your phone

  • Run sheets, addresses, customer names as they appear on the sheet, notes, your logbook and what DropRun learns about your driving are saved only on this phone.
  • DropRun has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and nothing is sent to us.

What leaves your phone

  • Drive times: the locations of your stops are sent to the OSRM routing service (router.project-osrm.org) to work out how long each drive takes.
  • Address search: when an address isn't on the offline map, the address text is sent to OpenStreetMap's search service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org).
  • Road closures: DropRun asks QLDTraffic for current events using your API key. Your location is not sent.
  • Updates and maps: DropRun downloads updates and road and address data from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • Anything you share: images, logbook exports and sample files leave the phone only when you send them. A sample file contains the text read from your sheet, which can include names and addresses.

Location

  • DropRun uses your location during a run, to show where you are on the route, measure kilometres and arrival times, and check the spot where you deliver.
  • When you tap Delivered, your position is saved as that house's spot only if you're within about 250 m of its pin. You can skip this with \"Delivered – don't save GPS\".
  • When you scan a sheet, DropRun takes one reading to find the nearest store.
  • DropRun doesn't track your location when no run is running. While a run is on, a notification stays visible, and you can finish or abandon the run at any time.

Other permissions

  • Microphone: only used for voice answers, and only if you turn that on in Personalise.
  • Camera: takes photos of run sheets. Photos are kept in the app's temporary storage until Android clears them.
  • Notifications: run alerts, such as a late drop or a customer to call.
  • Full-screen alerts and drawing over other apps: used only to bring DropRun to the front when you arrive at a drop, if you allow it.
  • Installing updates: DropRun downloads new versions and asks you before installing them.

Deleting your data

  • Clearing the app's storage in Android settings deletes everything DropRun has saved on your phone.
