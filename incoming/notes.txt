Digging now follows how dolly trucks are really loaded. Rows are counted from the door by crate count (part-full row at the door or the back, set per vehicle). To reach a drop, every row in front of its deepest crate has to be cleared, costed per crate moved. A row that has been cleared or emptied stays out of the way. New per-vehicle setting for how strongly the planner avoids going deep (real time, mild or strong). Replaces the tower model from 2.0.35.
